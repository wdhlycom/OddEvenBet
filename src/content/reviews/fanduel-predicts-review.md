## What FanDuel Predicts actually is

An event-contract app that sits apart from the FanDuel Sportsbook, built with CME Group and executed by Plus500, which handles brokerage and clearing. It is a prediction market in structure — yes/no contracts on financial, economic and sports events — with a compliance pedigree that is unusually strong for the category.

## Fees

The fee is simple: 2% of potential payout, including early closes, and nothing extra on losing trades. Deposits and withdrawals are free with a $10 minimum. The catch is that "potential payout" scales with the odds: on a heavy favorite the payout can be a multiple of your stake, and 2% of that is a meaningful drag on what is already a thin-margin position.

## Coverage: the split that matters

The "nationwide" claim needs reading carefully. Financial and economic contracts are available in all 50 states — genuinely national. Sports contracts are not: they are restricted to roughly 18 states that don't already have FanDuel sports betting. If your interest is sports and you live in a big betting state, you likely do not have access to what you came for.

## Licensing

CFTC regulation plus CME Group's involvement is the strongest compliance story in this list. That said, event contracts can still lose 100% of principal, and nothing about the branding changes that.

## Who this is wrong for

If you want sports contracts and live in a state with FanDuel sports betting, or you trade heavy favorites where 2% of payout stings, this is a poor fit. If you're in a non-sports-betting state or already use FanDuel and want financial event contracts, it is a credible, low-friction option.
