## What Coinbase Predict actually is

Cash-settled prediction contracts inside the Coinbase app, running through Coinbase Financial Markets / Derivatives and listing Kalshi contracts. On top of that sits the headline feature: short-dated crypto-price contracts, including 15-minute windows.

That short-dated crypto angle is the only genuinely unique product in this entire group. It is also the most dangerous one, and the two facts are connected.

## Fees and the transparency problem

The fee is a combined Kalshi exchange fee plus a Coinbase brokerage fee, and the honest version of this sentence is that you do not see the total until the order confirmation screen. You can form a view of the Kalshi side, but the Coinbase add-on is not itemized up front the way a clean per-contract fee would be.

For a product that markets itself on convenience, that is a real gap. Cost should be visible before you click, not after.

## The crypto-price risk

A 15-minute crypto-price contract settles on a price that can move violently in either direction inside a single news cycle. These are not the relatively calm political markets that anchor the rest of the category — they are near-pure short-term volatility, and losing your full stake is a routine outcome, not a tail risk.

Treat these as speculation, not as prediction. The time horizon is too short for anything resembling analysis.

## Licensing and coverage

Coinbase Predict is CFTC-regulated through its registered derivatives and FCM/DCM structure, with Kalshi supplying the contracts. It is US-resident only, and Nevada is blocked outright — worth checking if you live there.

## Who this is wrong for

If you want full fee disclosure before you commit, this is not your platform. If short-dated crypto volatility would keep you awake, the 15-minute contracts are the wrong product for you regardless of the fee.

If you already hold a Coinbase account, want USD/USDC settlement, and accept that the crypto-price contracts are high-variance by design, the integration is smooth — just demand the fee number before you send the order.
