## What DraftKings Sportsbook actually is

One of the largest sportsbooks in the United States, built on a market regulated state by state. The product's strength is breadth: if a market exists in American sport, it is probably here, with depth.

The menu is the central argument. No one in the regulated sector competes consistently with the combination of market count, live betting and same-game parlays that DraftKings offers.

## Odds: the headline misleads

Here is the nuance a bettor should hold on to. DraftKings promotions — "bet $5, get $200" and the like — advertise odds that, when you look at the detail, are usually shorter than the headline suggests. They are acquisition hooks with conditions, not pure value offers.

That does not mean the regular odds are bad: in a regulated, taxed market, they are competitive within their context. The lesson is to read the detail of each promotion before assuming it is better than it is.

## Same-game parlays

The flagship product. The depth of the same-game parlay builder — combining bets from a single match — is among the best in the sector. It is at once its biggest draw and its biggest trap: parlays, by design, have worse expected value than single bets, and the ease of stacking legs encourages exactly that.

## Deposits and payments

The minimum deposit is low, $5, which lowers the entry barrier. Withdrawals go through bank, card and PayPal, within the standard timescales of the regulated market. Funds protection is backed by state licences, a real advantage over offshore operators.

## Licences and the honest warning

DraftKings operates under state-by-state licences in the US, not a single global licence. That means real player protection, but also that availability is strictly geographic: it is no use to readers outside the United States, and within the country it depends on the state.

One last nuance remains: as across the whole sector, there are reports of restrictions on accounts that win consistently. It is not exclusive to DraftKings, but it is worth knowing.

## Who it is not for

If you are outside the United States, this review does not apply to you. And if you are inside the US and your plan is to chase promotions, read the fine print: DraftKings' real value is in the menu and the execution, not in the odds in the adverts.
