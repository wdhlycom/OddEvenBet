## What PredictIt actually is

The oldest name in US political prediction markets, born out of academic forecasting. It lists binary "yes/no" contracts on elections and policy events, and the price functions as a probability. Media and researchers have quoted it for years, which tells you what it is best at: a public gauge of political likelihood, not a betting venue.

## Fees

Winning trades pay 10% of net profit. That is high for the category — most competitors charge low single digits or fee the spread instead. The minimum deposit is about $5, so entry is cheap, but the economics punish anyone actually trying to build a position.

## The $850 cap

Each market is capped at $850 of exposure per person. That is the single most important constraint: it caps both your upside and your downside, and it makes PredictIt useless as a place to deploy real size. It is protection-by-design, and also a hard ceiling.

## Licensing and continuance risk

PredictIt is not a registered exchange. It operates under a CFTC no-action letter — administrative forbearance, not a license. That distinction matters: it has survived for years on it, but the legal basis can be revisited, and its continued existence is not guaranteed the way a licensed venue's is.

## Who this is wrong for

If you want size, diversification beyond politics, or a licensed operator, this is the wrong tool. If you are a politics obsessive or researcher who wants a transparent probability signal and accepts the 10% fee and $850 cap, it remains the reference in its niche.
