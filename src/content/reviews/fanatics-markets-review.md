## What Fanatics Markets actually is

A standalone prediction market app from Fanatics, running on Crypto.com's federal-level CFTC infrastructure. It is deliberately separate from Fanatics Sportsbook, so the audience is not the sportsbook bettor but the broader Fanatics customer — the person who already buys gear, collects cards or uses the casino.

The product is event contracts like the rest of this category. The reason to pick it over a cheaper venue is the rewards layer, not the pricing.

## Fees

Contracts run roughly 2.4¢ each way across the 20–80¢ price band. That is meaningfully more expensive than the sub-1¢ taker fees at DraftKings or Robinhood, and it adds up quickly if you trade often.

The $5 minimum deposit is the lowest in this group, which lowers the barrier but should not be mistaken for cheap trading. Entry is cheap; the per-trade cost is not.

## FanCash and whether it matters

FanCash rewards run up to roughly 10% and, unusually, pay out win-or-lose. That is the honest differentiator. But the value is only realized if you spend it somewhere in the Fanatics ecosystem — the sportsbook, casino, collectibles or store. If none of those interest you, the reward is a coupon for a store you do not visit.

Weigh the reward against the fee honestly: a 10% kickback does not rescue a 2.4¢-per-contract cost unless the reward itself is something you wanted anyway.

## Licensing and coverage

CFTC-regulated through the Crypto.com infrastructure, US-only and 21+. Coverage is roughly 24 states plus some territories — one of the narrower footprints in this list. The 21+ age gate also removes younger users entirely.

## Who this is wrong for

If you are outside the ~24-state footprint or under 21, it is not available to you at all. If you want the cheapest possible contract, the fee is a clear reason to look at DraftKings or Robinhood instead.

If you are already deep in the Fanatics ecosystem and will genuinely spend FanCash elsewhere, the win-or-lose rewards are a legitimate reason to choose it over a cheaper venue.
