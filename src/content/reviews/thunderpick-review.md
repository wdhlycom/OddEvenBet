## What Thunderpick actually is

A crypto-first betting site launched around 2017, focused on esports but with traditional sports and casino as a complement. The entire money flow runs through cryptocurrencies: BTC, ETH and USDT are the main ones. There is no fiat layer underneath.

That defines who feels comfortable here. If you already live in crypto, sign-up is light and money moves at the speed of the chain. If you expect a linked bank account, this is not your place.

## Odds and commissions

On the main esports titles — CS2, Dota 2, Overwatch — the book is deeper than most hybrid books, and the odds are competitive. On traditional sports it is competent but not a differentiator.

Deposits and withdrawals carry no internal commission: you pay the network fees of whichever chain you choose. That is honest, but it is worth remembering that a congested network can make a small transaction expensive.

## Bonuses: the fine print matters

The welcome bonus looks aggressive, and that is where you should slow down. Every bonus is tied to wagering requirements (rollover) before you can withdraw. It is not a gift: it is a discount conditional on you playing through the amount several times. Read it before accepting, because unlocking it is not trivial.

## Deposits and payments

The real strength. Deposits arrive as soon as the transaction has enough confirmations, and withdrawals are paid on-chain — without the usual 3 to 5 day fiat cushion. For an esports bettor who wants their balance now, this is a genuine advantage.

## Licences and the honest warning

Thunderpick operates under a Curacao offshore licence. That means an authority exists, but with lax supervision and little practical capacity for a player in dispute to claim. It is not a regulator you can write to with serious expectations.

Add to that a second risk many overlook: your balance is in crypto, so its value in local currency fluctuates. You can win the bet and still watch the purchasing power of your withdrawal fall with the market.

## Who it is not for

If you want a strong licence, fiat deposits, or an operator that responds when something gets stuck, look elsewhere. Thunderpick is for those who already accept self-custody and offshore risk as the price for speed and anonymity.
