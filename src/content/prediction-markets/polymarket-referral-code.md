The answer up front, so you don't have to scroll: **Polymarket has no traditional "sign up and get $XX" promo code.** It's an order book that matches trades, not a gambling operator that pays bonuses to acquire users. The "Polymarket promo code" and "referral code pays out" hits you'll find are mostly marketing-site lead-gen — click through and there's no official real-money reward.

## 1. Why there's no sign-up bonus

Traditional books issue sign-up bonuses because they sit on the other side of your bet — your losses are their revenue, so they're happy to "give" you a little first to keep you around.

Polymarket is different: it doesn't bet against you, it only matches you with other traders and earns fees. It has no incentive to take a cut of your losses, so it also has little incentive to pay out bonuses to win new users.

## 2. Are those "referral codes" real?

Three cases:

- **Pure lead-gen**: headline says "get $100", click through is a review, and the code gives you nothing after signup. Most common.
- **Referral rebate program**: Polymarket did have a referral mechanism — you refer a friend, the friend trades, you get a small cut of their fees. But **that's a rebate to the referrer, not a sign-up bonus for the referred**, and there's a volume threshold.
- **Official promotions**: occasionally incentives tied to specific or new markets, but not a standing, universal promo code.

So if your plan is "enter a code at signup and get a reward", you can mostly give up.

## 3. Where you actually save

Instead of hunting promo codes, learn Polymarket's fee structure — that's the long-term saving:

- **Maker orders are free**: post a limit order for someone else to fill, and there's no fee. To save, prefer limit orders over market orders.
- **Taker orders pay**: fill someone else's order and you pay, highest near 0.50 and tapering toward both ends.
- **Rebates**: some markets or periods pay a rebate for providing liquidity.

One line: **the way to save is "post (maker)", not "find a code".**

## 4. If you see a code "guaranteeing money"

It's likely one of three risks:

1. A phishing link fishing for your wallet authorization.
2. A redirect to some other platform, not Polymarket at all.
3. A "referral rebate" dressed up as a "sign-up reward", to mislead.

The test is simple: **Polymarket has never put a "enter promo code" entry on its homepage.** No entry, no universal promo code.

## What this page should leave you with

1. Polymarket has no "sign-up money" promo code — don't fall for lead-gen headlines.
2. The referral rebate goes to the referrer, not to the new user as a bonus.
3. The real saving is the fee structure: post limit orders, use fewer market orders.
4. If you see a code that "guarantees money", suspect phishing or lead-gen first.
