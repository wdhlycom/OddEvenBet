## How to read this list

Two of the six scoring dimensions do most of the work for a beginner: **fees** and **clarity of the resolution rules**. A venue with a hundred markets and ambiguous settlement language is worse for a first account than one with twenty markets where you can read exactly what triggers a payout.

Rankings below are generated from the same scores shown on each review page, so they cannot drift apart.

## What to check before you deposit

1. **Read one full resolution rule.** If you cannot say precisely what makes the contract pay $1, do not trade it.
2. **Check the spread, not the price.** A 2-cent spread on a 50-cent contract is 4% you pay on entry.
3. **Confirm your jurisdiction is allowed.** This is the step people skip, and it is the one that costs them the balance.
4. **Verify before you need to withdraw.** Identity checks at withdrawal are the top cause of multi-day delays.

## Common first-account mistakes

- **Treating a high price as safe.** A contract at 0.95 still loses everything 5% of the time, and the payout is 5 cents on the dollar.
- **Confusing volume with liquidity.** A market can trade heavily and still have a wide spread at the moment you want to enter.
- **Ignoring the exit.** You can sell before resolution — many beginners never realise this and hold to settlement unnecessarily.
