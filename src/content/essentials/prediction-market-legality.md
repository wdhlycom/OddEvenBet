This article doesn't name any platform and doesn't give legal advice. It only lays out the principles, so you know what questions to ask and who to verify them with.

## The core question: what is it classified as

Whether prediction markets are compliant is rarely a simple "legal" or "illegal." The real question is: in your jurisdiction, does the regulator treat it as a **financial product** or as **gambling**.

- If it's a financial product, it falls under securities or derivatives rules — requiring licenses and disclosure.
- If it's gambling, it falls under gambling rules — with entirely different age, geography, and licensing requirements.

The same "bet on the rate hike" contract can be a compliant financial product in place A and prohibited gambling in place B. The answer follows the jurisdiction, not the product.

## Age limits

Common thresholds are **18** or **21**, but the exact number varies by region, and some places are a **total ban** — there's no "reach this age and you're allowed" path.

So don't just parrot "the US is 21" or "Europe is 18." Age limits are tied to the classification above: if a place treats it as gambling, it may clamp age and access down even harder.

## What you should do

Before you trade, check these three things:

1. **Verify the current rules in your region.** Use official sources, not forums and "I heard somewhere."
2. **Confirm the platform has a regulator you can name.** Where a platform's <details class="term"><summary>license</summary><span class="term-note">The operating permit a platform holds in a given jurisdiction — it determines who regulates it and whom you go to when something goes wrong.</span></details> comes from, and who oversees it, should be written clearly. If you can't name the regulator, be extra cautious.
3. **Don't use someone else's identity or bypass georestrictions.** Registering with borrowed ID or using tools to pretend you're in another region can itself be a violation — and nobody has your back if something goes wrong.

## What this page should leave you with
