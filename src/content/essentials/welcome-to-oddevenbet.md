## Who we are

OddEvenBet is an independent information site focused on **prediction markets** and **sports betting**.

First, what we are not:

- We're not a betting platform — you don't place bets here.
- We're not a bookmaker — we never take the other side of your money.
- We don't bet for you, and we don't hand you "sure win" signals.

We do one thing: **explain prediction markets and sports betting clearly.**

---

## What's on this site

The site has a few sections, and you can browse by need:

- **Learn**: where you are now — starting from zero, explaining every concept in plain language.

- **Prediction Markets**: betting on "real events" like politics and macroeconomics, e.g. "who wins the election." This is our deepest area so far.
- **Niche Sports**: betting on smaller leagues and niche sports.

- **Major Sports**: betting on the big ones — soccer, basketball, and so on.

- **Reviews**: our hands-on, scored evaluations of platforms.

There are also a few pages worth reading: **About**, **Methodology**, **Disclosure**, and **Responsible Gambling**.

## How to use the Learn section

Learn is built for people who know "completely nothing." It's split into four parts, which you can read in order:

1. **Getting Started**: get to know this site and nail down the basic concepts.
2. **Betting Basics · Concepts**: what words like odds, price, and contract actually mean.
3. **Betting Basics · Strategy**: why most people lose money, and how to avoid the traps.
4. **Risk & Mindset**: this is our signature. We don't teach you "how to win" — we teach you how to think.

Anyone can learn the rules. What actually separates people is cognition and mindset.

## Our position (stated up front)

We don't encourage anyone to bet.

But we respect one fact: life is full of choices, and choices carry risk. Buying a house, starting a business, switching jobs, even getting married — they're all, in essence, bets placed under uncertainty.

Our job is to help you **see the risk, do the math, and manage your bankroll** — not to push you into gambling.

> Whatever you read, remember the single iron rule: **only stake money you can afford to lose.**

## How this site makes money

When you sign up for a platform through a link on our site, the platform may pay us a commission.

That doesn't change what we write. We recommend a platform because it deserves it, not because it pays more. For the details, see [Disclosure](/disclosure).

## A reminder

Prediction markets and sports betting face different laws in different countries and regions. Many places have age limits (usually 18 or 21), and some ban them outright.

Before you decide to participate, confirm the rules where you live first.
