## How to read this list

No single sportsbook is "best" for everyone. What you want might be the strongest licence, the best odds, crypto convenience, or the fullest markets for one niche sport.

So instead of one blanket number-one, we **group recommendations by need** and give you the one or two books most worth looking at in each case. Across the four dimensions, **licensing and reputation carry the most weight** — what a beginner should fear most is not slightly worse odds, but not getting their money back.

## Mainstream sports (soccer, basketball, tennis)

This tier is about big names, stability and a strong licence:

- **bet365**: one of the biggest books in the world, with the widest markets and broadest coverage; strong on soccer, basketball and tennis. The default first choice.
- **Pinnacle**: famous for sharp odds, high limits and not limiting winners, so it suits people who do the work. No sign-up bonus, but over the long run the odds edge is more real.

## Crypto betting

If you do not want to go through a traditional bank and want speed and a low barrier, look at crypto books:

- **Stake**: the largest crypto sportsbook, relatively stable.
- **BC.Game**: heavy promos, many tokens, flashier bet types.

(For a detailed comparison of the two, see [How to choose a crypto sportsbook](/niche-sports/stake-vs-bcgame).)

## Niche sports (esports, darts, snooker)

The big books focus on soccer and basketball; niche-sport markets are often fuller at specialist books:

- **GGbet**: an esports specialist with deep CS2, Dota 2 and LoL markets.
- **Pinnacle**: a good reputation across niche sports, no negative-balance policy; suits people who do the work.

## Three traps beginners should avoid

1. **Looking only at the sign-up bonus.** Books with the biggest bonuses often claw the profit back later. Check the licence and reputation first.
2. **Ignoring withdrawal speed.** Being able to win but not withdraw is the same as not winning. Check the withdrawal record before you commit.
3. **Treating a crypto book like a bank.** Crypto books are convenient but weakly regulated — do not leave more than you can afford to lose.

## What this page should leave you with

1. There is no "best" — only the book that fits your needs.
2. What a beginner should look at first is licensing and reputation, and only then odds.
3. For mainstream sports look at bet365 and Pinnacle; for niche sports look at GGbet and Pinnacle; for crypto look at Stake and BC.Game.
4. Protect your bankroll first, then worry about odds.
